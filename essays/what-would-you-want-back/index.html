What Would You Want Back?

What do people living with spinal cord injury most want back?

The headline number
681 people with SCI ranked seven functions; arm and hand function led for tetraplegia

Ask anyone what they would want back after a spinal cord injury, and most say walking. Then ask the people who live with one.

In 2004, Kim Anderson asked 681 people living with spinal cord injury to rank seven functions by how much getting each back would improve their lives. The lists split by injury level: arm and hand function led for people with tetraplegia.

The interactive essay lays out both lists in the survey’s own order of first-place votes, and asks what research funding would look like if it followed them.

Sources: Anderson KD. Journal of Neurotrauma. 2004.

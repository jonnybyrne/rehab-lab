The $50 Hand

Can a $50 printed hand stand in for a $40,000 myoelectric one?

The headline number
$50 in materials against a $40,000 total device price

A volunteer movement prints prosthetic hands for about $50 in plastic, materials only, against myoelectric devices with a $40,000 total price.

It started small and spread by map: one YouTube comment thread drew 70 pins in six weeks.

The essay weighs what the printed hand can actually do, including the harder numbers on how often prosthetic devices are rejected by the people they are made for.

Sources: Schull & Homan. CHI. 2016. Biddiss & Chau. 2007.

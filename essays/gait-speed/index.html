How Fast Do You Walk?

What does your walking speed say about your health?

The headline number
Each 0.1 m/s faster: hazard ratio 0.88 for mortality (34,485 adults, 9 cohorts)

Temperature, pulse, breath, blood pressure, oxygen saturation: five vital signs are recorded without thinking. In 2009, Fritz and Lusardi made the case for a sixth, the speed you walk at.

Studenski and colleagues pooled nine cohort studies: 34,485 community-dwelling adults, all 65 or older, each timed on a short walk and followed for years. Each 0.1 meters per second of faster gait speed was associated with lower mortality, a hazard ratio of 0.88.

The essay has you take the vital yourself, in one hallway with one stopwatch, then shows what the number can and cannot say.

Sources: Studenski et al. JAMA. 2011. Fritz & Lusardi. Journal of Geriatric Physical Therapy. 2009.

Second Place Is a DNF

How far can you run when the race ends only when everyone else stops?

The headline number
124 loops, 516.67 miles, stopped together: no winner (October 2026)

A backyard ultra has no finish line: run the same 4.167-mile loop every hour, on the hour, until one runner is left. Everyone else, however far they ran, is recorded as a DNF.

At the October 2026 World Team Championship in Belgium, Merijn Geerts and Ivo Steyaert stopped together after 124 loops, 516.67 miles, and there was no winner.

The essay follows the format’s strange arithmetic of quitting, and the physiology research on what sleep deprivation and extreme exertion do to the runners still standing.

Sources: De Pauw K, et al. European Journal of Sport Science. 2024. Race result as reported in the essay.

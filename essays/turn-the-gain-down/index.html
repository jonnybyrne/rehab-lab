Turn the Gain Down

Why does a spastic muscle fight a fast stretch and allow a slow one?

The headline number
25 in 100 develop spasticity after stroke (pooled, 23 studies)

Spasticity is a stretch reflex with its amplifier turned up: a fast stretch fights back, a slow one is allowed.

Pooled across 23 studies, 25 in 100 people develop spasticity after stroke; with weakness the figure was 39.5 in 100, and severe spasticity in about 9 in 100.

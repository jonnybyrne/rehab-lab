The Spring That Has to Arrive on Time

The same brace cut the energy cost of walking for four patients and did nothing for three. Why?

The headline number
Energy cost improved 12 percent as a group; 3 of 7 patients got no benefit

A spring-like ankle foot orthosis bends while the foot is on the ground, stores energy the body put into it, and returns part of that energy at push-off. If the return lands at the right moment, walking can cost less. If it lands at the wrong moment, the same brace can do nothing at all.

Seven patients already wearing prescribed polypropylene AFOs improved their walking energy cost by 12 percent as a group, and three of the seven got no energy benefit at all. Their braces measured a mean stiffness of 0.19 newton meters per degree.

A later study of spring-like carbon AFOs found the brace taking over ankle work in ten patients. The essay fits the spring with you and asks what decides who benefits.

Sources: Bregman DJJ, et al. Prosthetics and Orthotics International. 2010. Bregman DJJ, et al. Gait & Posture. 2012.

The Muscle Microphone

What does a muscle say when it should be saying nothing?

The headline number
Motor unit potentials measured in milliseconds; a 200 microvolt amplitude floor

An EMG is a listening exam. A needle microphone sits in the muscle and reports what it hears: silence, static, single voices, and crowds.

Its measurements are tiny and exact. Motor unit potentials last a few milliseconds; Preston and Shapiro print a tighter duration range of 5 to 10 milliseconds and a 200 microvolt amplitude floor, and the essay shows both printings.

Learn to hear the four things the microphone can tell apart, and a resting muscle stops being quiet in the ways that matter.

Sources: Preston DC, Shapiro BE. Electromyography and Neuromuscular Disorders, 4th edition. 2020.

The Most Expensive Steps

Why do some steps cost so much more oxygen than others?

The headline number
Walking after stroke: 1.5 to 2 times the oxygen cost of typical gait

Every step has a price, measured in oxygen, and some gaits pay far more than others.

After stroke, walking costs 1.5 to 2 times the oxygen of typical gait in matched comparisons, at comfortable speeds of 0.67 to 0.79 meters per second.

The essay prices a room of gaits side by side, from typical walking to braces and prostheses, and shows why the price, not just the pattern, decides who can keep walking.

Sources: Macko et al. Stroke. 1997. Waters & Mulroy. Gait & Posture. 1999.

Did carbon-plate shoes really break the marathon?

How much of the marathon revolution is the shoe?

The headline number
About 4 percent lower metabolic cost in the lab (n = 18 elite men)

One shoe made running about four percent cheaper, metabolically speaking: in the lab, a carbon-plated racer cut the metabolic cost of running by about 4 percent in 18 elite men.

A later model put the saving at 2.6 to 3.4 percent, depending on speed.

Nine years after the lab result, the two-hour marathon fell in a real race. The essay traces what the shoe actually did, and where the usual telling goes off the rails.

Sources: Hoogkamer W, et al. Sports Medicine. 2018. Kipp S, Kram R, Hoogkamer W. Frontiers in Physiology. 2019.

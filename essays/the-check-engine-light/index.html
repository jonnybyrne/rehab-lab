The Check-Engine Light

Why is a kinked catheter a blood pressure emergency after spinal cord injury?

The headline number
20 mmHg systolic rise: the line that defines an episode

After a spinal cord injury high in the back or neck, a problem below the injury can push blood pressure up fast. That reaction is autonomic dysreflexia, and the headache is the warning lamp.

A rise of 20 mmHg in systolic blood pressure is the line that defines an episode. The usual trigger sits below the injury: a bladder that cannot empty accounts for 75 to 85 percent of cases.

The Bladder Loses Its Instructions

What happens to the bladder when the cord stops sending instructions?

The headline number
40 cm H2O: the detrusor pressure line that marked kidneys at risk

The bladder works like a switch with two settings: store urine, or let it out. A spinal cord injury does not damage the bladder itself; it cuts the wires that run the switch.

The number that guards the kidneys is pressure. In the classic urodynamic study, sustained detrusor leak point pressure above 40 cm H2O marked the upper urinary tract at risk.

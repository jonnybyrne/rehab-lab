Which blood thinner fits which patient after joint surgery?

The headline number
PEP trial: 43 versus 18 deaths from pulmonary embolism (13,356 hip fracture patients)

Nine ways to keep a still leg from clotting, shelved like a formulary. In the PEP trial, 13,356 hip fracture patients received aspirin or placebo: 43 people in the placebo group died of pulmonary embolism, and 18 in the aspirin group.

For elective joint replacement, EPCAT II (3,424 patients) tested five days of rivaroxaban followed by aspirin 81 mg once daily against continued rivaroxaban.

Wind-Up

Why does the tenth identical stimulus hurt more than the first?

The headline number
Wind-up threshold: repetition at about 0.33 Hz; the tap never changes, the answer grows

Give the same painful tap again and again, fast enough, and the spinal cord answers bigger each time. The tap never changes. The answer grows. That is wind-up.

The phenomenon appears once stimuli repeat at about 0.33 Hz or faster, the rate Mendell and Wall found in 1965.

In fibromyalgia, the same summation is reached with less input: in the titration study the essay rebuilds, the fibromyalgia trace reached the top rating at 49.9°C against 52.2°C in controls (Staud et al., 2007). Pain is not a wire; the cord turns the volume up or down.

Sources: Mendell LM. Frontiers in Pain Research. 2022. Staud R, Robinson ME, Price DD. Journal of Pain. 2007. Rolke R, et al. Pain. 2006.

The headline number
Emergency visits nearly doubled, 2020 to 2022; admissions more than tripled

Emergency department visits for pickleball injuries nearly doubled from 2020 to 2022, and admissions more than tripled.

But the player base grew just as fast, so the rate per player barely moved.

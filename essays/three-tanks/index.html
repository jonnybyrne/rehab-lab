Three Tanks

Where does the energy for a hard effort actually come from?

The headline number
About 75 seconds: the crossover where aerobic and anaerobic contributions meet

Three systems remake the ATP every contraction spends. One responds almost instantly, one supplies rapid energy through glycolysis, and one increases its output as oxygen use rises. All three contribute from the start.

The shares are not fixed: at a maximal exercise effort of about 75 seconds, the aerobic and anaerobic systems derive roughly equal shares of the energy.

The essay runs one hard effort through all three tanks and watches the shares change second by second, then asks what the tanks mean for a body that walks with them after stroke.

Sources: Gastin PB. Sports Medicine. 2001.

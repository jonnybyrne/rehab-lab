Where Does the Rehab Day Actually Go?

Inpatient rehab is famous for three hours of therapy a day, so where do the other hours go?

The headline number
Alone 60.4 percent of the day; with a therapist 5.2 percent

Inpatient rehabilitation is famous for one promise: three hours of therapy a day. But a rehab day is much longer than three hours, and nobody hands you a map of the rest of it.

One way to get that map is to watch. In the stroke-unit observations the essay rebuilds (58 patients completed the study, of 64 recruited), patients spent 60.4 percent of the observed day alone and 5.2 percent of it with a therapist, and over half of it resting in bed.

A second ward, watched years later, kept a different ledger, including a therapist present for 29 percent of observations. The essay runs one ward clock from 8 AM to 5 PM and audits where the hours went.

Sources: Bernhardt J, Dewey H, Thrift A, Donnan G. Stroke. 2004. Adey-Wakeling Z, et al. 2021.

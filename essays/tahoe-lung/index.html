Tahoe Lung

Why do so many Tahoe 200 runners finish the race coughing?

The headline number
Tahoe 200, 2022 report: 240 starters, 101 did not finish

It is not a diagnosis. It is trail slang for what 200 miles of dusty, pollen-thick, cold mountain air does to lungs breathing hard for days.

The 2022 race report counted 240 starters and 101 who did not finish. The course starts at about 6,234 feet and climbs over 10,000.

June is the basin’s pollen month: one pine can release up to 5 pounds of pollen in two to four weeks. The essay separates the folklore from the measurable ingredients.

Sources: Leonard L. Tahoe 200 report. run-ultra.com. 2022. Destination Trail. Tahoe 200 race listing. 2026.

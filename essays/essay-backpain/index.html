If you had no back pain, what would your MRI say?

What would a spine MRI show in someone with no pain at all?

The headline number
80 of 100 pain-free 50-year-olds show disk degeneration on MRI

In people with no back pain at all, disk degeneration appears in 80 of 100 scans by age 50, and a disk bulge in 60 of 100.

An earlier study found a disk bulge at one or more levels in 52 percent of 98 adults without back pain.

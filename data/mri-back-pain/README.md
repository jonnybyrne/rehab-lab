# Data for Essay 08 — The $50 Hand

Chart values used in the essay, extracted from the page. Every value below appears verbatim in the essay; nothing was invented.

## Files

- `cost_bars.csv` — materials-cost comparison behind the thin-$50-bar scroll gag.
  Source: 3D Printing Industry ("Video: A $50 3D Printed Prosthetic Hand", Jose Delgado Jr.) for the $50 figure; top-end myoelectric range as stated in the essay.
- `pediatric_cost_grid.csv` — lifetime-cost grid behind the pediatric recomputer: one device price × refit interval from age 5 to 18.
  Sources: 6–12 month refit cycle from Orthopedics This Week (UNM / Carrie Tingley Hospital); $700–$10,000 conventional pediatric hand from TechX Lab; $30–$50 printed from UTA FabLab. Device prices $5k/$10k/$40k are the essay's comparison tiers.
- `pediatric_cost_default_series.csv` — the default series the recomputer shows ($50 device, 12-month interval): cumulative cost at each age 5–18.
- `grip_bars.csv` — peak grip forces behind the evidence section.
  Sources: X-Limb (253 g, 21.5 N, PLoS ONE 2020, DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0232766); Touch Hand 4 (30.4 N, MATEC Web of Conferences 2022); adult-male reference 510 N (Portuguese grip-strength norms, PLoS ONE 2020, DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0236555).
- `abandonment_bars.csv` — mean rejection rates from Biddiss & Chau 2007 (Prosthetics & Orthotics International, DOI 10.1080/03093640600994581), which screened ~200 articles and reported rates in 40.
- `pin_bloom.csv` — step labels of the (now removed) pin-map bloom animation: 1 / 8 / 70 / 140+ pins. Stylized, not geographic; 140 was the animation cap, not an exact count.

## Date accessed

October 10, 2026 (the essay's "data last checked" date).

## Transformations

- `pediatric_cost_grid.csv`: devices = ceil(156 / interval_months) over ages 5–18; total = devices × device_cost_usd. Computed from the page's own formula; no new data points.
- `pediatric_cost_default_series.csv`: step function generated by the page's formula for the default settings ($50, 12 months): one $50 device at each whole year from age 5 to 17, flat to 18.
- All other files are direct transcriptions of values stated in the essay.

## Gaps

- The wrist-flexion simulator's finger geometry is drawn procedurally in the page's JavaScript (fixed segment lengths and per-finger curl angles), not from a dataset, so there is no CSV for it.
- The e-NABLE scale figures (~20,000 volunteers / 8,000 recipients; 197 chapters / 53 countries / ~33,000 volunteers) are dated organizational claims with no audited count; they are noted in the essay's footnotes, not charted.

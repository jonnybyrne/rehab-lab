# Chart data — Essay 11, "Whose Pain Gets Believed"

Every figure below appears in the essay's page source and traces to the
published study named beside it. No values were invented, rounded, or
recalculated. The full verification record (designs, sample sizes, PMIDs,
confidence intervals, and limitations) is in the essay research memo,
prepared 2026-10-12; every figure was read in the source abstract or
full text.

## Files

- `ledger.csv` — the thirty-year ledger (Essay 11, Audit Entry 08): year,
  study, population, finding, and the headline gap measure.
- `waits.csv` — median minutes to first analgesia for the two waiting-room
  comparisons (Audit Entry 03).
- `prescribing.csv` — the percentage-based findings used in the identical
  chart, the national ledger, the kids section, and the protocol switch.
